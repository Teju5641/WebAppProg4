Lakshmi Tejaswini Davuluri presents plays by Ibsen

Hedda Gabler

Act 1 - A short time later we resume

MRS. ELVSTED

[Looks at Hedda.]

Back to him?

HEDDA

Of course.

MRS. ELVSTED

I shall never go back to him again.

HEDDA

[Hedda rises and approaches her.]

Then you have left your home—for good and all?
